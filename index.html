HOW TO READ THIS

Geometry first. Mission decisions later.

Horizons gives each body’s apparent direction at the selected surface point. We compare that direction with a skyline computed from LOLA elevation samples on the Moon’s curved surface. This version uses a local 10 km terrain window. It does not include distant ridges, solar-array orientation, Earth’s partially visible disk, antenna constraints, or a radio link budget.
